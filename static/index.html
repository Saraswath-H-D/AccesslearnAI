Empowering Accessible Learning with Artificial Intelligence

AccessLearn AI automatically transforms standard educational audio and video lectures into accessible formats—including timestamped transcripts, plain-language summaries, text-to-speech audio, and Braille (.BRF) files.

Universal Accessibility Formats

📝

Whisper Transcription

Accurate timestamped transcripts enabling deaf and hard-of-hearing students to read along and jump to specific timestamps.

✨

AI Content Simplification

Converts complex academic jargon into clear plain-language explanations with helpful real-world analogies.

🔊

Text-to-Speech Audio

Synthesizes lecture notes into playable MP3 audio files with customizable playback speed and seeking controls.

⠿

Braille Ready Format (.BRF)

Translates lecture text into Grade 1 & Grade 2 Braille code formatted for refreshable braille displays and embossers.
